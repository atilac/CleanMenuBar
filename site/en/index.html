Why it exists

macOS 27 rebuilt the menu bar as a single window and broke Hidden Bar, the app I used to hide icons. It relied on a technique that stopped working.

CleanMenuBar is derived from Hidden Bar with an adapted mechanism, built and measured directly on macOS 27 Golden Gate one day after its public release (14 September 2026).

What it does

  • Hide and revealA click, a global shortcut, or just hovering.
  • Always-hidden sectionA second span that stays hidden even when you expand.
  • Auto-collapseAfter 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds.
  • No permissionsNo Accessibility, no Screen Recording. Runs sandboxed.
  • Nine languagesEnglish, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Russian.
  • Collects nothingNo network, no telemetry, no accounts.

Setting up

  1. Hold ⌘ and drag the icons you want hidden so they sit to the left of the CleanMenuBar separator.
  2. Anything to the right of the separator stays visible at all times.
  3. Click the arrow to collapse or expand, or press ⌃⌥⌘C from anywhere.
  4. Once arranged, turn on Hide the separators to leave only the arrow.

Positions survive relaunching — macOS remembers where each icon sits.

Installing

Download the .dmg, drag CleanMenuBar to Applications, and open it.

The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarised by Apple, so it opens without warnings. Until it reaches the Mac App Store, the download above is the official way to get it.
